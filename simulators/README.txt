A SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE IN THE SKY - INTERACTIVE DEMOS
========================================================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Double-click index.html. It opens in your default browser (Safari or Chrome both work).
2. Click a big button (or press 1 to 5) to open a demo. Every demo has a
   "Back to menu" link at the top left.
3. For full screen, press F, or press Ctrl + Cmd + F on a Mac. Press F again (or Esc) to leave.

KEEP ALL THE FILES TOGETHER IN THIS ONE FOLDER (the menu links to the others).

WORKS OFFLINE
Everything is inside the files. There is no internet, no logging in and nothing to install.
The only things that need internet are the optional "Sources" links in demo 5, which
open the original documents in your browser.

USB KNOB / KEYBOARD
A USB rotary knob normally acts as the arrow keys (or + and -). Click once on the page
first so it has keyboard focus, then turn the knob:
  Demo 1 Cascade chain:  Left/Right (or + / -) change the storm strength Kp by 0.1;
                         Up/Down change it by 0.5.
  Demo 2 Warning clock:  Left/Right move the clock back/forward a little; Up/Down move it more.
  Demo 3 Sky view:       Left/Right move the time of day by 5 minutes; Up/Down by 1 hour.
  Demo 4 Drone fleet:    Left/Right move time by 30 seconds; Up/Down by 10 minutes.
  Demo 5 Timing holdover: moves whichever tab is open: tab 1 the time without satellite
                         timing; tab 2 the countdown; tab 3 Left/Right the time since
                         timing was lost, Up/Down the length of the outage.
Stepping with the knob pauses any automatic playback. Mouse and touch still work as normal.
If the knob is a volume dial and does nothing, set it to "arrow keys" mode, or use a
cheap rotary-encoder knob that sends keyboard keys. Mac volume keys are not seen by
web pages.

WHAT IS MEASURED AND WHAT IS NOT
Each demo keeps its own coloured labels: MEASURED (my data), PUBLISHED, ARGUED or
ILLUSTRATIVE. Timing effects in demo 5 are argued, not measured.

TIP FOR THE STAND
Close other tabs, turn off notifications, plug the Mac into power, and test the knob
before judging starts.
