A Single Point of Failure in the Sky - interactive demos

Space weather as a common-mode threat to satellite navigation and satellite broadband. Choose a demo.

1Cascade chain
Press a button and watch one flare or storm spread from the Sun to navigation, phone masts, farms, the power grid and more.
2Warning clock
How much warning do you get? A solar flare gives none; a storm gives hours to days. Replay real events.
3Sky view
See which satellite navigation satellites a real storm disturbs as they cross the sky above a receiver.
4Drone fleet
Real measured position error on a fleet of drones: one shared cause takes them all off course at once.
5Timing holdover
How long can phone masts, substations and trading clocks keep time if satellite timing is lost? (Argued, not measured.)
Works offline. Press 1 to 5 to open a demo, or use the arrow keys and Enter. F = full screen. Inside a demo, arrow keys or a USB knob adjust the main control.
