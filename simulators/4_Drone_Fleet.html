One storm, every drone: why a shared cause is so dangerous

Each drone replays real measured GPS position error (RTK, real-time kinematic, the centimetre-accurate kind) from a reference station, 30 seconds at a time.

Time when every drone was off course at once
MEASUREDEach drone's error is real horizontal position error from a fixed reference-station pair, worked out with RTK software from public GNSS (global navigation satellite system) data, post-processed in 30-second steps (the first 10 minutes of each record, the receiver start-up, are skipped); Kp from GFZ Potsdam. Shared cause: all drones replay the same moment, as if they share one sky and one correction station. Unrelated faults: each drone replays a different random moment of the same record, as if its faults had nothing to do with the others. ILLUSTRATIVENo real drones were measured: these are station receivers, none in Northern Ireland. The drones, the formation and the ring size (error, stretched scale) are only a picture of the numbers. The time is UTC.
